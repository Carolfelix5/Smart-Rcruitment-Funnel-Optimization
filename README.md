# Smart Recruitment: Funnel Optimization for Time-to-Hire Reduction

## Problem
NovaTech Solutions, a mid-size IT services company hiring 200+ tech roles/quarter, faced a 48–55 day time-to-hire against a 25–30 day industry benchmark, driven by
manual resume screening and manual interview scheduling — resulting in ~30% candidate drop-off before interview.

## Approach
End-to-end BA lifecycle covering business case development, stakeholder elicitation,
AS-IS/TO-BE process design, root cause analysis, requirements documentation
(BRD/FRD/Business Rules), Agile delivery (User Stories, JIRA, Sprints), and full UAT/defect management through to solution evaluation.

## Impact
- Time-to-hire: 48–55 days → 27 days (~49% reduction)
- Candidate drop-off: Approx 30% → 13%
- Recruiter capacity: +32%
- 1 critical UAT defect caught (PII/ranking data exposure in candidate notifications) before go-live

## Tools Used
MS Word, Excel, JIRA, Draw.io,Lucid Chart, BABOK-aligned BA techniques (Stakeholder Analysis, 5 Whys, Fishbone/RCA, MoSCoW, RTM)
